How to use this page

Six steps. About a minute. Go through it with your player.

  1. 🔎
    Find your player

    In the menu, tap Find your player, then your child's name. You'll see every series: where they line up and when they rest.

  2. 📣
    Call a play like Coach

    Under the field, pick Offense or Defense, the play (Boom or Sonic), and the call. The field shows it.

  3. #️⃣
    Tap your player's number

    Spots are #1 to #5. Tap one (or tap the player on the field) for their job.

  4. ▶️
    Press Play

    Watch the play move. Slow motion is on to start.

  5. 🔊
    Tap "Read it to me"

    Go through the steps together, out loud.

  6. 🏡
    Practice 5 minutes

    Use the drill on the card, in the yard or at the park.
