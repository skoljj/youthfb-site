For players and parents. Watch the play, find your spot, and practice together.

Parents
How to use this page (1 minute)
  1. Find your player by name to see their spot (or break) in each series.
  2. Call a play like Coach: Side (Offense or Defense), Play, Call, Target, Direction, Cadence.
  3. Tap your player's spot number (Coach gives every kid a number, #1 to #7).
  4. Most games are 5 v 5 (#6 and #7 sit out). Pick 6 or 7 players for a bigger game.
  5. Press Play and watch the play move. Slow motion is on to start.
  6. Tap "Read it to me" and go through the steps together.
  7. Practice 5 minutes in the yard with the drill at the bottom of the card.

Tip: the page remembers what you picked in the web address. Save it or text it to another parent.
